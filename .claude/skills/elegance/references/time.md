# Time: future, current, past

Time is a node's station in the loop ([theory §2.2](theory.md#22-time-as-the-station)): read to act, written from observation, or written from action. A sentence does not carry its station, so it has to be marked or gated. A station is one column of the grid, read as `cells/?<station>-*.md`.

## Table 2 — Time (future, current, past)

| Time | The question it answers | Place in the loop | Confidence means | Drift looks like | Corrected by | Stale form |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A · Future | What could be, what we expect, and what we decided to make so; the decision is a check mark on the node | read to act: a disagreement with the world is settled by acting, or withdrawing | standing: was it decided, by whom, still wanted; before the decision, feasibility: do the premises hold | the world failed to follow, the will lapsed, or the premises changed | do the work, withdraw the commitment, or retire the option | a zombie intention or a dead option |
| B · Current | What is believed true at this scope now | written from observation: settled by looking again | evidence: was it verified, and how recently | the world changed and the node did not | re-observe; edit or delete the node | a stale fact |
| C · Past | What happened | written from action: settled by the record | fidelity of the record | the record diverges from what happened | the record system, usually version control | a falsified history |

*Future is the only row where a node can be an expectation or a commitment; the check mark separates them.*

## Which station is this?

Ask how a disagreement with the world would be settled: by acting or withdrawing, **A · Future**; by looking again, **B · Current**; by consulting the record, **C · Past**. The shopping-list test in [theory §2.2](theory.md#22-time-as-the-station) settles hard cases.

## Marking the station

**Mark the station, or gate it.** Where a gate makes what is and what should be coincide, present tense is honest; where none exists, the same sentence hides drift as fact. A future node names who decided it and when it lapses, or reads as an expectation; a current node points at what was observed and when; a past node lives in the record system, or names the record it derives from.

## Along time

**Cost concentrates in the future.** A wrong future node is acted on, becomes a wrong world, then a faithful record of the wrong action; a wrong current node misleads only until someone re-observes; a wrong past node is the most silent, its cost lost learning. **Excess concentrates in the current:** the record and the plan have a natural size, but a description has none, so the current is where prose grows unbounded and where the fewest-elements test bites hardest.
