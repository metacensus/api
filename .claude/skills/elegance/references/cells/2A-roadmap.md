# 2A · Roadmap

Tier 2 [**Organization**](../scope.md) · [**Future**](../time.md) · above [1A vision](1A-vision.md) · below [3A design](3A-design.md) · across [2B identity](2B-identity.md), [2C org history](2C-org-history.md).

## Example

The next efforts in order, each with an appetite and a decider, and the outcome they add up to.

## Location

**Roadmap**, in the [location key](../grid.md#location-key).

## Clear when

The efforts listed are the ones decided, in the order decided, each with its appetite, its decider, and the claim in [1A](1A-vision.md) or [1B](1B-problem.md) it traces to, and none past its date; confidence is the standing of that decision.

## Excess

- **An effort described rather than named.** One line: the effort, its appetite, its decider, its claim. The design is [3A](3A-design.md). Test: reduce the effort to that one line — if you cannot, the design has leaked in and belongs in 3A.
- **Justification for the order.** The order is the decision; the reasoning was the proposal's.
- **Efforts that have landed, still listed.** They leave for [3C](3C-release.md). Test: check each effort's status — a landed one leaves for 3C.
- **A roadmap that is really a vision.** The world is [1A](1A-vision.md); this is the next few steps toward it.

## Cost

A year of efforts in the wrong order or at the wrong claims.

## Moves

Reorder, re-scope, or retire. Extend with one effort carrying its appetite, decider, and claim; a claim deliberately unaddressed gets a one-line deferral. Until a roadmap exists, the honest form is a hole named as a hole.
