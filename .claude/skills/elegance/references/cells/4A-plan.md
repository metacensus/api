# 4A · Plan

Tier 4 [**Module**](../scope.md) · [**Future**](../time.md) · above [3A design](3A-design.md) · below [5A criteria](5A-criteria.md) · across [4B surface](4B-surface.md), [4C merges](4C-merges.md).

## Example

A parent item with its children, its non-goals, a decider and a decide-by date.

## Location

**Git/Issues**, in the [location key](../grid.md#location-key).

## Clear when

The children are the decomposition, each workable without reaching up and each with binary criteria; non-goals, a decider, and a date are present; the tracker offers structure only, so the decider's standing is the confidence.

## Excess

- **A parent that restates its children.** The children are linked; the parent holds what they share and nothing they each hold. Test: cover the parent and read the children — what stays true only in the parent is what it holds; the rest was a copy.
- **Design in the plan.** The target lives in [3A](3A-design.md); the plan links to it and sequences the work.
- **Non-goals padded into a rationale.** A non-goal is one line naming what is out and, at most, why. Restraint is stated, not argued.
- **A child with a paragraph of context.** A child that needs context to be worked is a signal the parent is unclear, not a reason to copy the parent down. Test: try working the child without reaching up — if you cannot, fix the parent, do not copy it down.

## Cost

Weeks. Scope creeps across children, and slack is built rather than absorbed.

## Moves

A child that reaches up rewrites the parent. Split children; add the missing criterion or non-goal as one line. Close as done, not done, or deviated ([theory §4.3](../theory.md#43-keeping-the-loop-closed)), which is the alteration that writes [4C](4C-merges.md).
