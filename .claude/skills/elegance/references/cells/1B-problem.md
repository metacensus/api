# 1B · Problem

Tier 1 [**Field**](../scope.md) · [**Current**](../time.md) · above [0B rules](0B-rules.md) · below [2B identity](2B-identity.md) · across [1A vision](1A-vision.md), [1C field history](1C-field-history.md).

## Example

The problem as it stands in the world, and why it persists.

## Location

**Org Docs**, in the [location key](../grid.md#location-key).

## Clear when

It matches what stakeholders experience, every claim carries a source that says what it is claimed to say, and it says why the problem persists; an expert who does not work here would sign it.

## Excess

- **A claim and its paraphrase.** Sourced claims, once each. Test: for each claim, look for a later sentence saying it in other words — keep the sourced one, cut the echo.
- **Evidence summarized at length.** The claim, the source, one line on what the source shows.
- **The solution creeping into the problem.** The problem stands on its own; the answer is [1A](1A-vision.md) and [2A](2A-roadmap.md). Test: read each sentence and ask if it states the world or the answer — an answer belongs in 1A or 2A.
- **History mixed in.** How the field got here is [1C](1C-field-history.md).

## Cost

Solving a problem that is not there. Every tier below aims wrong.

## Moves

Rewrite a claim to what the sources now say; retire a claim whose sources have gone. Connect each claim to its evidence, and extend only with a sourced claim.
