## Example

The rules the organization lives by: how it writes, what counts as the source of truth, how it decides.

## Location

**Org Docs**, in the [location key](../grid.md#location-key).

## Clear when

Each rule is crisp, general, and non-contradictory, a reader can name the action it forbids, and the tiers below pass their checks because of it; confidence is review by someone with standing, or the gate a rule has become.

## Excess

The tier where excess is hardest to see, because every sentence sounds principled. This skill sits here and is not exempt.

- **A rule that forbids nothing.** If no situation triggers it and no action violates it, it is decoration. Delete it or sharpen it until it can be broken. Test: name the action it forbids — if you cannot, it is decoration.
- **A rule stated, then explained, then illustrated.** The rule and one exemplar. The explanation is the theory's, linked; the illustration is a pointer at a node that already passed. Test: cut the explanation and the example — if the rule still forbids what it forbade, they were residue.
- **The same rule in two documents' words.** One source, the other a link. Where two repos each state it, one is the source and the other says so.
- **A principle that has become a gate, still kept as a sentence.** The gate refuses; the sentence is residue.
- **Reading order, rationale, and history in the rules themselves.** A handbook says what the rule is. Why it was adopted is the proposal's record in [0C](0C-retired-rules.md); how the handbook is organized is one line at its top.
- **A hole list that grows without closing.** A named hole is one line at a known scope. A list of a dozen is a backlog misplaced, and belongs in the tracker.

## Cost

The highest in the graph. Every derived node inherits the bias, felt as years of drift, never as an event.

## Moves

Rewrite the rule whole with the old wording gone; make it a gate ([theory §4.2](../theory.md#42-where-information-goes)) and retire the sentence; retire a rule that has stopped firing. Never extend here directly — a missing rule enters as [0A](0A-proposal.md) and lands here by decision. Everything below connects to a rule here by name; nothing restates it.
