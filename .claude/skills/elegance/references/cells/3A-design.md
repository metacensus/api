## Example

The target architecture, and the plan to reach it within appetite, with non-goals stated.

## Location

**Design Docs**, in the [location key](../grid.md#location-key).

## Clear when

The structure stated matches what is being built, boundaries are binary questions with their near-misses named, the plan fits the appetite, and someone with standing reviewed it; every delta against the running system since is recorded.

## Excess

- **A design that restates the contract.** The contract is generated or typed in [3B](3B-contract.md); the design says what the system is for and where its boundaries fall. Test: cover the paragraph and read the types in 3B — nothing lost means it was a copy.
- **Alternatives rejected, at length.** One line each: what, and the cost that ruled it out.
- **The system described from the outside in prose.** If the diagram or the types say it, the paragraph beside them is a copy. Test: hide the diagram — if the prose adds no fact it did not show, cut the prose.
- **Non-goals argued rather than stated.**
- **A design kept after it was reached.** It retires into [3B](3B-contract.md), where the contract now lives.

## Cost

A quarter of divergence across teams. Seam drift that no single diff shows.

## Moves

Strengthen where possible — a contract as generated types is a gate, a boundary as a binary question is checkable. Rewrite when the system deviates and the deviation is accepted; otherwise the alteration goes to the system. Add a delta or a non-goal as one line, never more description; a hole is marked, not filled.
